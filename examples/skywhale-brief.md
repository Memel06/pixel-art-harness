# Skywhale Observatory

An original flying whale carries a small observatory town over an ocean of clouds.
The README hero should communicate scale, material shading and editable scene
construction at a glance. No generated or third-party reference image was used.

- Native canvas: 320×208, opaque, side view with shallow architectural depth.
- Palette: 24 declared colors, cool teal whale against dusty violet and peach sky;
  warm ivory reserved for windows, lanterns and celestial light.
- Composition: forked tail at left, rounded head at right, town above the spine;
  small traveler and distant sailing islands establish scale.
- Eight functional layers separate atmosphere, body, flipper, terraces,
  architecture, travelers and foreground.
- Four 180 ms frames; stable whale body and city, two-pixel flipper movement,
  pennant flutter and drifting lanterns. This is ambient motion, not a swim cycle.
- Deliver scene JSON, rebuild script, native Aseprite, sheet, metadata and previews.
- Review v1 versus v2: break up hard sky-band boundaries and add cloud cap highlights.
  Intermediate renders were removed after final review.
